nexus-matcher  |  semantic schema-field matching, PythonPyPI v2.2.0
  • Maps Avro, JSON Schema, and SQL DDL fields onto data-dictionary entries by meaning rather than string equality: P@1 0.581, Recall@10 0.878, 364 fields/sec on CPU across 688 labelled pairs (BIRD-SQL 361, OMOP CDM v5.4 327).
  • Kept the auto-approve gate conservative: at the 0.87 threshold it approves 85 of 688 fields (12.4%) at 95.3% precision and routes the rest to a human. The 22.9 MB wheel bundles its own int8 ONNX encoder, so it installs air-gapped with no model download and no torch.
  • Retracted an earlier P@1 of 0.715 after tracing it to token-identity leakage in the OMOP split, and published the retraction in the repository README.
